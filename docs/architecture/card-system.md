# Echo in Ink — Card System Blueprint

## Purpose

Cards are not UI containers. They are atmospheric frames that introduce worlds, systems, and transformations.

The card system supports Studio, Identity, Systems, Worlds, Lumo, Archive, and future Echo in Ink experiences.

## Core Card Archetypes

### Transformation Cards

For identity direction, narrative architecture, and creative services.

Tone:

- Editorial
- Human
- Spacious
- Reflective

### Systems Cards

For frameworks, AI systems, identity tools, and design infrastructure.

Tone:

- Structured
- Intelligent
- Archival

### Featured World Cards

For immersive experiences such as Lumo and major case studies.

Tone:

- Cinematic
- Narrative
- Atmospheric

## Shared Rules

Cards should use:

- Deep ink surfaces
- Subtle glass borders
- Generous spacing
- Restrained glow
- Editorial typography

Avoid:

- Pure black
- Excessive effects
- SaaS-style layouts
- Dense information blocks

## Motion

Motion should feel environmental:

- Slow
- Atmospheric
- Gravitational

Never playful or overly interactive.

## Principle

Every card should feel like a fragment of a larger world.
